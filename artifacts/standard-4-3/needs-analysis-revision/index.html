Standard 4.3 · Artifact One

Listening to Educators

Needs-Analysis Revision Record

Trevor Arcadipane · Prepared September 26, 2026

Purpose: Use feedback to improve how I learn about educators’ formative-assessment needs. This is a revised planning resource, not a report of staff responses.

From feedback to revision

In EDEL 590 Discussion 5.1, I proposed a short survey about formative assessment. A peer asked whether the first question would guide the choice of tools and whether it would be open-ended or multiple-choice. My instructor also asked about the response format. This record summarizes that feedback and presents my revised questions; it is not a transcript of the discussion.

Revised planning survey

Suggested invitation: I’m planning practical support with quick checks for understanding. Please share what is useful in your classroom and where support would help. Skip any question you prefer; do not include student names or individual student records.

1. How do you currently check understanding?

Using the responses

  1. Summarize the educator’s priority and ask whether I understood it correctly.
  2. Agree on one lesson goal before choosing a tool or example.
  3. Match support to the preferred format and identified access needs.
  4. Set a follow-up to review the task and the educator’s feedback.

Evidence to retain after use: the agreed goal, the educator’s chosen support, the revision made, and a short reflection on what helped. Public portfolio evidence should omit identifying information.

Current result: clearer questions and a more specific plan for listening. Staff responses, a completed coaching cycle, and effects on teaching or learning remain to be collected.
